## Generation prompt

Create an original pixel-art character and weapon DESIGN SHEET for a single-player 2D top-down action roguelite game. This is a cohesive exploratory game-art concept board, not a finished animation atlas. Landscape composition, dark neutral charcoal background, restrained elegant spacing, sharply legible sprites. Authentic hand-placed pixel art: crisp square pixels, nearest-neighbor appearance, limited color ramps, no anti-aliasing, no smooth vector shapes, no painterly brushwork, no 3D rendering. Each character should visually read like a carefully designed 32x48 or 48x48 low-resolution sprite enlarged in integer steps.

Design ONE original adventurous young adult dungeon explorer, gender-neutral appearance, short tousled dark hair, expressive face visible, short teal scarf/cape that does not obscure the body, ivory undershirt, charcoal/light leather armor, warm brown belt and boots, tiny copper clasp, fingerless gloves. Compact readable silhouette with oversized head and clear separated limbs, subtle top-down three-quarter camera view (see top surfaces, NEVER isometric ground). Practical nimble action hero, appealing but not copied from any existing game. Clear teal player identity visible against dark dungeons, small ivory/copper accents. No animal ears, no giant hat, no ornate clutter.

Layout:
LEFT upper half: large primary three-quarter front-facing idle hero sprite, full body, clean silhouette; nearby smaller consistent FRONT, BACK, SIDE orthographic sprite views, SAME character/outfit/proportions. All views unarmed so the held weapons can remain separate assets.
LEFT lower half: three small action key-pose studies of the SAME hero: running with scarf trailing, low forward dash, and a slashing pose holding the dagger. Show clear limb placement and pixel motion streaks sparingly.
RIGHT half: four generously spaced standalone weapon sprites with the SAME pixel density and coherent copper/teal-steel palette. Show enlarged icons with tiny normal-size duplicates to communicate small-screen readability.
1 DAGGER: short asymmetric steel blade, teal wrapped grip, copper small guard, nimble and compact.
2 SWORD: medium straight double-edged steel blade, simple copper crossguard and dark grip, balanced and clearly longer than dagger.
3 GREATSWORD: huge broad slab-like steel blade with one clipped corner, reinforced spine, long two-handed handle, heavy readable shape, not ornate.
4 EVOLVED DAGGER: unmistakably the same dagger upgraded, split teal luminous channel running through blade, restrained two-tone pixel energy edge implying a piercing wave. It must still read as a dagger, NOT turn into a sword.
Use only short clean English pixel-font labels: EXPLORER, FRONT, BACK, SIDE, RUN, DASH, SLASH, DAGGER, SWORD, GREATSWORD, EVOLVED DAGGER. No other text. Include a small 8-color palette strip at bottom. Strong visual hierarchy, ample gutters, no overlap, no scenery, no UI panels, no logos or watermarks. Original IP, do not emulate or reproduce a named game's character or weapon designs.
