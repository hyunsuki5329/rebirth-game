# M10 — Rebirth and World Threat

## Implementation
Hub Rebirth consumes a data-defined cost (initially 100 Gold, then 100 × next Count), requires a prior victory, grants one point and updates a configured permanent Gold multiplier. The initial multiplier curve is 1, 2, 2.7, 3.4, with its final increment continued beyond the authored entries. This is tuning data, not a fixed design formula. Unlocks and purchased upgrades survive Rebirth. Rebirth and purchases are rejected during a run or scene transition.

Vitality costs one point and adds 20 maximum HP on the next fresh player. Focus costs one point and adds 0.25 seconds to the allowed interval between combo actions. Neither mutates ScriptableObject data. Hub buttons show effects and ownership. Result UI separates raw Base Gold from bonus-adjusted earnings.

Threat follows Rebirth Count. Threat 1 and later replace a ranged enemy's single arrow with three arrows at -20, 0 and +20 degrees, preserving its existing telegraph and attack cadence. A separate 1.2× Threat Gold factor applies alongside the permanent multiplier and Momentum. Enemy HP is unchanged by this feature.

- Three fresh-profile sessions: passed. Each used Bow, Safe Route, actual enemy attacks and accepted player attacks, physical WASD/Shift movement and legal Dash requests. No reward grants, direct enemy kills, health edits or combat teleports were used. Reward selection and room advancement use their normal APIs after clearing; walking between rooms and menu time are excluded. Time scale was 3 for execution speed.
