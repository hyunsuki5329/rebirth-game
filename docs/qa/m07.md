# M7 — Dangerous Shortcut

## Implementation
The choice room has a green rectangular Safe exit and an orange diamond Dangerous exit, with labels showing room count, base Gold, ground-blast risk and rare-stack chance. Safe crosses two ordinary rooms; Dangerous crosses one elite room. Both converge at the Boss entrance. Old rooms and callbacks are removed on transition; repeated route flags cannot return to the unchosen branch.

ShortcutRules configures elite Gold x2.5, safe rare chance 20%, elite rare chance 70%, rare reward 2 stacks, and ground-warning timing/radius/damage. Rewards use a deterministic seed and are granted once. Pending choices block departure; capped stacks become Gold once per excess stack.

Elite enemies add ground attacks at the player's marked position. Warning precedes damage by 0.7 seconds; the area does not follow the player. Leaving the marked area avoids damage. Owner death and room/new-run cleanup remove hazards.

## Validation
- Two physical exits, distinct color/shape, actual danger trigger into Elite and uncleared-room lock.
- Warning has no early damage, one hit per active hazard, dodge, owner death and new-run cleanup.
- Seed/chance bounds, rare two-stack application, stack cap conversion and duplicate choice rejection.
- Six real-combat runs with identical Bow and pre-branch item selection: three Safe, three Dangerous. Each reaches Boss entrance, records duration/HP/death/Gold, checks room counts, reward deduplication and fixed route choice.
- Review found no code blocker; its requested rare-stack integration coverage was added.
- Initial physical-exit test detected a near-zero generated BoxCollider2D. Builder now explicitly assigns its size; collider size and traversal are regression-tested.

## Human validation

Automated comparison uses stationary Bow attacks at nearest enemies and a 3x simulation timescale; it is evidence for that strategy, not a claim of human playtesting or general balance.
