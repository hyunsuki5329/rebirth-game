# M3 — Enemy Combat

## Implementation
- Chaser approaches then performs a telegraphed melee attack.
- Ranged approaches/retreats to a distance band, telegraphs, then fires a projectile.
- Charger pauses to telegraph a locked direction, charges, then has a recovery window.
- EnemyAI subclasses, EnemyAttack, EnemyHealth and EnemyMotor2D separate behavior, attack, health and physics. EnemyData/AttackDefinition assets own tunable parameters; no runtime state is stored in them.
- Player/Enemy/World/Hitbox/Projectile layers are named. Projectile collision handles teams, world obstruction, lifetime and owner death.
- EnemyArena contains all three distinct prefabs with original placeholder colors and attack range indicators.

## Regression findings
- Ranged immediately restarted attacks rather than retreating. Attack initiation now requires being in its preferred distance band.
- A single projectile hit two overlapping Hurtboxes for 16 rather than 8 damage. A consumed flag now prevents duplicate damage before deferred destruction.
- Charger active range visual stayed behind while damage moved. The active visual now follows the charging body; the windup direction remains fixed.
- A diagnostic test initially moved a Rigidbody without synchronizing its transform, preserving the old aimed direction. Corrected test setup and reproduced the actual duplicate-hit defect. Production targeting was not altered to accommodate that test error.
- Independent review identified the charge visual issue; all reported important M3 issues have regression coverage.
