# M8 — Boss

## Implementation
Rift Warden uses immutable EnemyData/BossPatternData with 160 initial HP. It alternates a direction-locked telegraphed charge and three marked ground areas with staggered explosions. Each pattern ends in a stationary recovery window. Boss entry locks the exit, and the HUD shows HP and current phase.

RunConclusion resolves once at the end of the frame. Player death takes priority over boss death in the same frame. The result blocks player actions and removes player/enemy attack effects before notifying listeners. Victory settles the room reward; full result/Hub settlement follows in M9.

## Validation scope
- Boss room has one living boss and a locked exit.
- Repeated charge/blast patterns, locked direction, measured recovery duration and absence of active hazards during recovery.
- Actual accepted attacks from all four weapons against the active boss during recovery windows; the test uses scripted repositioning and does not prove ordinary-player movement proficiency.
- Same-frame player/boss death resolves to Death once; victory leaves no damaging effects.
- Earlier M7 tests cover ground warning delay, single hit, leaving the marked area and owner-death cleanup.

MANUAL_PLAYTEST_REQUIRED — normal movement/Dash avoidance, readability, perceived reaction windows and boss difficulty. Placeholder boss artwork currently reuses a larger tinted boar sprite; unique final boss art is not part of this milestone.

A separate test uses the actual PlayerDash/PlayerMotor2D physical movement to avoid a locked charge and land a Bow counterattack.
